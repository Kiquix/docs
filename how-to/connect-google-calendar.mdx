title: "Connect Google Calendar"
description: "Connect Google Calendar so Fred can write scheduled bookings to your calendar."
---

Use the Google Calendar integration when Fred should write scheduled bookings to Google Calendar.

## Connect Calendar

1. Click your avatar in the left sidebar.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Open **Integrations**.
4. Click **Available apps**.
5. Find **Google Calendar**.
6. Click **Connect Google Calendar**.
7. Complete the Google authorization flow.

After authorization, Fred returns you to **Integrations**.

## Use Calendar in scheduling

Calendar write access is connected from **Integrations**. Event sync behavior is managed from the study scheduler.

For User Sphere studies, open the study **Scheduler** and review the Google Calendar availability and event sync options available to your account.

<Note>
  Fred uses Calendar for scheduling support. Keep participant consent and meeting privacy in mind when sending calendar events.
</Note>
