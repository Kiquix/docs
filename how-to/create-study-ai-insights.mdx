---
title: "Create study insights with AI"
description: "Generate AI-assisted insights for one study."
---

Study AI insights summarize evidence from a single study, including analytics, tagged evidence, and method context.

## Open the study analytics

1. Click **Projects** in the left sidebar.
2. Select the project.
3. Open the study.
4. Go to **Analytics**.

## Generate insights

1. Click **Study AI insights**.
2. Click **Generate study insights**.
3. Wait for Fred to finish processing.
4. Review the generated summary, findings, evidence coverage, and limitations.

If insights already exist, use **Regenerate study insights** when you intentionally want Fred to run the analysis again.

<Warning>
  **Generate study insights** can be unavailable when the study does not have enough completed or usable evidence yet.
</Warning>
