---
title: "Manage your subscription"
description: "Open billing settings and manage your Fred subscription through Stripe."
---

Billing owners can manage plan and payment details from **Settings**.

## Open billing

1. Click your avatar in the left sidebar.
2. Click **Settings**.
3. Go to **Billing**.
4. Click **Manage billing**.

Fred opens the Stripe billing portal.

## What you can manage

In Stripe, you can manage:

- payment methods
- billing information
- invoice history
- outstanding invoices
- subscription or plan details when available

Return to Fred when you are finished.

<Note>
  Billing controls depend on your role and subscription state. If you cannot see billing actions, ask the billing owner or team leader.
</Note>
